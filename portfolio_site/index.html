About

Final-year BCA Data Science, Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham — CGPA 7.67

I'm drawn to how things actually work under the surface — how people learn, how data behaves, how a small idea turns into something that runs unattended and doesn't fall over. Most of what's here started as a personal itch: too much time lost to manually checking job boards, an SEO audit tool I wanted to trust the output of, an expense tracker I kept rebuilding better.

Research experience

UNDER REVIEW

Academic Responsiveness to Structured Peer Support: A Predictive Profiling Study

Co-author · 2nd International Conference on Intelligent Computing and Knowledge Extraction (ICICKE 2026) · 2025–2026

Contributed to a multi-author quasi-experimental study on a structured peer-support intervention across three undergraduate cohorts (218 students) — psychological, behavioral, and academic effects.

  • One-way ANOVA, Bonferroni-corrected pairwise t-tests, and Cohen's d effect-size estimation across experimental and control groups
  • Pearson correlation, Random Forest importance ranking, and multinomial logistic regression (SciPy, Scikit-learn, Pandas) to profile pre-intervention predictors of academic responsiveness
  • Co-developed visualizations and interpretation of findings on motivation, self-efficacy, and performance across response profiles
